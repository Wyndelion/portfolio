Fight Guys

A physics-based auto battler that blends cuteness with brutal gladiatorial combat.

Genre
Auto battler, roguelite
Platform
Browser (HTML5)
Engine
Unity
My role
Developer, with Leo Schweiger
Context
Multimedia project, FH Salzburg
Music
Sebastian Schweiger, Andreas Steiner
Play in your browser ↗

You assemble a team of up to five bear-like fighters. Between rounds you buy units, modifiers, potions and food in a shop, then your team is sent into the arena against another player's team. The fights play out on their own and are driven by physics.

Every lost fight costs a life and every win earns a trophy. Strong runs climb toward an endless mode that only ends when your lives run out.

REVERIE

A 3D action roguelike where art forms both the setting and the gameplay space.

Genre
Action roguelike
Platform
Windows
Status
Prototype
Team
Team Graukas
Context
University project
Download the prototype ↗

You play a failed artist who ends up as curator of a mysterious collection. After restoring the gallery you step into its central painting, a portal into a world inspired by surrealism.

Combat is real time and built around a tree-based combo system: you chain inputs along the tree, and reaching an endpoint triggers a finisher with its own animation and effects. Between runs you visit the Masterworks, living paintings that grant upgrades. Each Masterwork is made by a different artist, and the idea is to give artists a platform to bring their own style into the game.

It was built under a tight university schedule. The team plans to build a new game around the same concept.

You are a TV shopping host selling products by typing words. Better words sell faster, and you have to keep typing so viewer retention never drops to zero, otherwise the show is cancelled.

Made for the 12th MMArmalade Jam, a game jam for students of FH Salzburg. The game is entirely in German.
